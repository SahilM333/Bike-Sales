This project analyzes bike sales data using Excel and Pivot Tables to uncover buyer behavior, purchase trends, and demographic insights. The interactive dashboard allows for easy exploration of key metrics like income, age, commute distance, and car ownership.

Key Features
Average Income Per Purchase – Compare income levels of buyers by gender.
Average Age of Purchase – Identify which age groups are most likely to purchase bikes.
Commute Distance vs Purchase – Understand how distance impacts purchase decisions.
Car Ownership Analysis – Explore the relationship between owning cars and buying bikes.
Interactive Dashboard – Pivot tables and charts make it easy to filter and analyze data.
Tools Used
Excel – Data organization, calculations, and analysis
Pivot Tables – Aggregation and summarization of sales data
Charts & Visualizations – Bar charts, line graphs, and comparative analysis
Insights
Middle-aged buyers are the largest group of bike purchasers.
Males tend to have higher average income per purchase than females.
Buyers with shorter commutes (0–1 miles) have higher purchase counts.
Car ownership influences bike purchase patterns.

This project demonstrates how Excel and Pivot Tables can be leveraged to generate actionable business insights from sales data
